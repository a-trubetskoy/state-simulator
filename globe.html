pick

      

      

        Ray-sphere unproject, then a lon/lat grid over the source rings. No
        picking buffer and no readback, so the tint keeps up through a drag
        instead of pausing for the gesture.
      


      

      
carve

      
      
      

        Armed, a drag draws a cut instead of turning the globe. Draw in one side
        of a county and out the other. By default the piece boundary IS the drawn
        line — tracts straddling it are split and their numbers divided by area.
        Ticked, tracts stay whole and each joins the side its centre falls on,
        which is what a preset naming tract GEOIDs has always meant.
